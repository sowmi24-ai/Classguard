ClassGuard

AI-Powered Student Accessories Compliance Detection System

How it works
  1. Capture: classroom camera, image or video
  2. Detect: AI model finds students, backpacks, laptops, phones
  3. Assign: each accessory is linked to the nearest student
  4. Check: rules decide PASS or FLAG (ID card required, phone not allowed)
  5. Alert: violation shown live and saved to a CSV report
